# main c9a51bf

A full bench of main `c9a51bf74` on every frame on the dev box rig, on 2026-11-27, during the third UX bench of the day. It is compared with the morning's full run of main `1ba47e0` (`20261127-102826`). Main in between is the second UX bench's fixes (frameos#1104–#1117): mostly samples, the scene list (opens on a whole row, round panels' columns in the middle of the circle, the Sticky's landscape column), the wasm runtime's error frame and `frameos.canvas` asking for room first (#1111).

- **Run:** `20261127-135642`, `bench.py run --rediscover --keep-booked --baseline 20261127-102826`. Every suite on every frame; the E1002 and E1004 sampled as usual.
- **Boards:** all twelve ESP32s run local builds of `c9a51bf` (`firmware/build.sh`, ELF `3869004d` generic, `c36f0581` 32 MB), flashed one at a time with `flash_checked.sh` (`firmware/flash-main-c9a51bf.sh`), all twelve verified.
- **Pico:** stays on `2026.11.12-main.e0a754bd0` (needs BOOTSEL; `embedded/pico` unchanged since).
- **Pis:** Cloud-W cross-built from `c9a51bf` (armv6, `release_main_c9a51bf74`, drivers the live release's: `driver_abi.nim` unchanged). Cloud-5: no route to host.
- **Backend:** the bench backend at `c9a51bf` (no backend changes since `1ba47e0`) with a wasm runtime built from it; frames 1–6 given the repo's samples (`update_scenes.py --apply`).
- **Verdict as printed:** 3 fail, 31 slow, 10 note. No crash.

## Fails

- **Sticky: Hacker News → Handwriting notes, then → Pixel art: no switch** (`scene slot 48 unreadable`, `slot-read-failed`; the frame stayed on Hacker News). Hacker News had left PSRAM in pieces: 1.3 MB free in blocks of at most 30 KB after the runtime's 1 MB emergency reserve was spent and armed again during its render; the slot read takes one block the size of the scene file. The runtime drops the outgoing scene before it builds the new one, but only after the C side had read the new file. Fix: frameos#1132 (+336 B; its full scenes suite on the Sticky passed, `20261127-152243`). Six HN → heavy-scene switches in a row did not reproduce it: the pieces come from the bench's 23 minutes of scenes before HN.
- **Cloud-W Files `render_timeout`:** the bench's warm render landed in the runtime's render-log pause (Files reopens on a video and renders every 50–80 ms). Bench fix: frameos#1124.

## Not regressions

- **Advance 7.0" score 3955 → 1475:** its RGB panel alternates a full render of an unchanged benchmark picture (raster 2.4 s) with a damage-only one (70 ms); each bench `render` lands on either and the run pairs lines across them (this run: raster "render 2419 / done 76"; the morning: "70 / 2437"). The true raster time is ~2.4 s (the SenseCAP's 480x480 is 2.2 s), so the earlier 4101/3955 were the inflated ones. To fix in the bench: a benchmark picture that changes every render, or the warm render's own `render #N` line.
- **E1003 Clock "fastest warm 96 → 240 ms":** the morning's 96 ms was one outlier among 234–345 ms (now 240–338).
- **SenseCAP / Cloud-W "Slideshow with motion":** the Ken Burns sample changed in frameos#1114 (the frames got it through `update_scenes.py`); it no longer animates there, so the numbers compare two scenes.
- **E1004 rotate 90 restart 76 s (47 s in the morning):** 75.7 s on 2026-11-26 too: one panel refresh more some of the time.
- **Kitchen sink "1 fps against 20" on every LCD:** the scene asks for one render a second on its other pages; the firmware's `render:animation` reported the interval as the period. Fix: frameos#1123.
