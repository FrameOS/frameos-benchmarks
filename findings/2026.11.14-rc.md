# 2026.11.14 candidate (main `ff6a5b9d5`)

A full bench of main at `ff6a5b9d5` (#950), the build the next release is cut from, on every board of the dev box rig on 2026-11-21. It is compared with the 2026.11.13 release (run `20261120-062810`).

- **Runs:** `20261121-152145` (Sticky, E1002, E1003, E1004, Pico) and `20261121-154133` (SenseCAP), both `bench.py run --rediscover --full --baseline 20261120-062810`. They were split in two because another session held the SenseCAP when the first started. Together they are one `--full` run of every board, and they are recorded as `full`.
- **Boards:** SenseCAP Indicator D1, reTerminal E1002, E1003, E1004 and Sticky on images built from `ff6a5b9d5` on the bench (`2026.11.13-main-ff6a5b9d5`, ELF `3004a294` generic, `6a4d0425` 32 MB). The Inky Frame 7.3 Spectra (Pico 2 W) is a thin client and stayed on `2026.11.12-main.e0a754bd0`: `embedded/pico` didn't change since 2026.11.13.
- **Backend:** the self-hosted backend at `ff6a5b9d5`, with a wasm runtime built from the same commit (`frameos/tools/build_wasm.sh`, emsdk 6.0.2) rendering the Pico's scenes.
- **Suites:** status, reboot, benchmark, scenes, builtin, input, desktop, code, storm, soak and offline on every ESP32; status and thin on the Pico. Nothing sampled.
- **Verdict:** **0 crash, 0 fail**, 17 slow, 22 note. 2026.11.13 had 0 crash, 4 fail, 29 slow, 28 note.

## Better

- **No crash and no fail on any board, in a full run.** The E1002's four Code-editor fails from 2026.11.13 (the dropped scene) are gone (FrameOS#925).
- **CPU +19 to +30 % on every board**, most of it the JSON loop, now **2 to 2.7× faster**. 2026.11.13 had lost ±10 % of that loop to code placement in a 16 KB instruction cache. FrameOS#924's 32 KB cache is the likely cause; I haven't bisected it.

  | board | 2026.11.13 JSON | main JSON |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | SenseCAP | 1,343 ms | 599 ms |
  | E1002 | 1,267 ms | 475 ms |
  | E1003 | 886 ms | 462 ms |
  | E1004 | 1,241 ms | 478 ms |
  | Sticky | 890 ms | 457 ms |
- **Graphics.** E1003 2584 → 6495: FrameOS#923 fixed its glyph cache misses, and UI warm renders went back to 160–230 ms (Counter 448 → 200, Home 387 → 155, Calculator 425 → 166). Sticky 1247 → 1608, SenseCAP 781 → 1050, E1002 718 → 1026.
- **SenseCAP:** most scenes switch 10–40 % faster (Kitchen sink 2,304 → 1,804 ms, Form showcase 2,504 → 1,804). The Code editor's list opens in 2.9 s instead of 18.1 s. The DVD logo animates at **27.5 fps** (21.8 on 2026.11.13). The bench missed it this run, because the summary every 10 s landed after its 8 s window; fixed in FrameOS#953.
- **Interpreter discards on the E1004 halved:** 105 to 56 over the same suites before the soak.

## Worse

### Internal RAM: 6 to 12 KB less on the boards with an SD card slot

The lowest internal RAM free since boot, after the benchmark:

| board | 2026.11.13 | main |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sticky | 30.1 KB | 20.2 KB |
| E1003 | 42.4 KB | 32.5 KB |
| E1004 | 38.5 KB | 26.8 KB |
| E1002 | 23.1 KB | 17.1 KB |
| SenseCAP | 13.7 KB | 13.9 KB |

This is FrameOS#924's trade: the 32 KB instruction cache costs 16 KB of internal SRAM, partly paid back by six static Wi-Fi RX buffers instead of the default. The E1002's −6 KB is that net cost exactly. The SenseCAP's floor didn't move; FrameOS#924 measured it there (9.8–10.8 KB over four boots), and why it holds where the others drop isn't known. The 32 MB boards lost 10–12 KB, a little more than the net, which is worth a look. Nothing in the run ran short: no failed allocation, no Wi-Fi or TLS error. The cache is also where the CPU gain comes from.

### Two scenes slower, both on the network

- **E1004 Wikimedia Commons:** fastest warm render 13.2 → 18.4 s. Its `get` (the photo's download and decode) is 12.8 s of it; the picture of the day changes daily. The scene also stamped its caption's border 16 times on every render (24,582 glyph draws, 2.4 s), as on 2026.11.13. Fixed in FrameOS#955: see "Follow-up".
- **E1002 Weather:** 2.2 → 3.3 s. `get` is 3.0 s of it.

## Unchanged, still worth knowing

- **The E1004's 38–43 s photo and clock scenes** are mostly the panel. A render is ~9 s, dither+pack 4.1 s and the automatic fit 0.75 s; the 13.3" Spectra's own refresh is the remaining ~25 s. FrameOS#954 takes ~2 s off the CPU part (see "Follow-up").
- **E1004 interpreter discards:** a render that decoded a photo frees its idle interpreters when less than 2 MB is left, and the E1004 has 1.0–1.7 MB with its 1200×1600 canvas. 65 in the soak. None held a UI's tap handlers, so no tap was lost.
- **The automatic fit runs on JSX and SVG pictures too** (`render/image` fits every drawn image on a palette panel): 0.75 s of every E1004 render of a clock or a UI. Whether a vector picture should be fitted at all is an open question for the owner.
- **SenseCAP HTTPS** skips startup for low internal memory, as on every build since 2026.11.10.
- **The Sticky's first Code-editor open waits ~6 s** beyond its 1.4 s render: the editor being installed. It was the same on 2026.11.13.

### The verification run of main `2032552e5` (with #952–#955), and a regression it caught

Runs `20261121-205145` (SenseCAP, E1003, E1004, Pico) and `20261121-210315` (E1002), both `--full`: **0 crash, 0 fail**.

- **E1004:** its photo and clock scenes are 2–3 s faster a render (Big typographic clock 39.1 → 36.9 s, Low Poly Animals 39.3 → 37.2 s): #954's pack and fit.
- **E1002: every render waited out the previous refresh.** #952 put the E1002 on a 565 canvas, and the rule that packs a colour panel's picture into its own canvas (made for the E1004, where it is what makes memory fit) then applied to it too. The canvas is lent to the panel for the whole ~25 s refresh, so a render that comes during one waits: render #128 was 7.4 s of render and 37.9 s end to end. Warm renders measured 27–29 s, the Code editor 25–37 s. FrameOS#963 packs in place only where the canvas and a buffer of its own would take more than half the PSRAM. Measured on the E1002 with #957 in:

  | E1002 warm render | ff6a5b9d5 | 2032552e5 | FrameOS#963 |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|
  | Big typographic clock | 5,565 ms | 28,920 ms | 3,216 ms |
  | Analog clock face | 4,769 ms | 27,706 ms | 2,151 ms |
  | Weather | 5,225 ms | 26,855 ms | 1,789 ms |

- **SenseCAP animations, measured for the first time** (#953's longer window): DVD logo 27.5 fps (target 30), Color cycling 20.2 (20), Slideshow with motion 11.5 (24, 86 ms a frame), Pixel toys 24.2 (50, 41 ms a frame). Kitchen sink's 1.9 fps came from a single summary that holds the scene's start; the bench should take the second summary when there is one.
