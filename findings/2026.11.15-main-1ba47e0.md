# main 1ba47e0

A full bench of main `1ba47e0e9` on every frame on the dev box rig, on 2026-11-27, after the second UX bench of the day: the twelve ESP32 boards (the six Elecrow boards and the AMOLED included), the Pico and Cloud-W. It is compared with the morning's full run of main `7d8d073` (`20261127-044049`). Main in between is the first UX bench's fixes (frameos#1090–#1102), most of them in samples and the wasm runtime. On the ESP32 the firmware changes are #1099 (a board's own event map follows the firmware) and the Nim of #1090, #1092, #1094 and #1097.

- **Run:** `20261127-102826`, `bench.py run --rediscover --keep-booked --baseline 20261127-044049`, with the bench code of frameos#1108 (key presses timed). Every suite on every frame; the E1002 and E1004 sampled as usual.
- **Boards:** all twelve ESP32s run local builds of `1ba47e0` (`firmware/build.sh`, ELF `f23c06e8` generic, `dd22069a` 32 MB), flashed one at a time with `flash_checked.sh`, all twelve verified.
- **Pico:** stays on `2026.11.12-main.e0a754bd0` (needs BOOTSEL).
- **Pis:** Cloud-W cross-built from `1ba47e0` (armv6, `release_main_1ba47e0e9`, drivers the live release's: `driver_abi.nim` unchanged). Cloud-5 did not answer over SSH (no route to host), as in the morning.
- **Backend:** the bench backend at `1ba47e0` (no new migrations) with a wasm runtime built from it; frames 1–6 given the repo's samples (`update_scenes.py --apply`).
- **Verdict as printed:** 2 crash, 9 fail, 35 slow, 9 note. The crashes are one intermittent watchdog restart on the Advance 7.0"; the fails are Cloud-W's VideoCore, which had wedged before the run (it passes after a reboot). See below.

## Findings

- **Advance 7.0": a rotation's restart woke by the RTC watchdog** (`rst:0x10 RTCWDT_RTC_RST`), both ways, 1.3 s after the Wi-Fi went down, where the morning's ended in a clean `RTC_SW_CPU_RST` 0.2 s after it. Not reproduced after the run: six plain restarts and two rotate-then-restart cycles were all clean. In the run it came after the desktop and code suites, with the board at about 1.4 MB of free PSRAM (0.96 MB at one point). Something in the restart's way down waits over a second when the board is short of memory; open.
- **Cloud-W video: every hardware path refused** (`VIDIOC_REQBUFS: Invalid argument`, software at 3–6.5 fps). dmesg: `vchiq_mmal_component_init: failed to create component -62`, the VideoCore wedge the 720p decoders cause (it had been up two days). After a reboot the video suite passes with no issue on this build.
- **5.79" e-paper: the first partial update after a full refresh waits 3.8 s** for the glass (`render:timeline` `update 3821.6`), the next one 0.5 s. In the morning the same open was a full refresh, which runs behind the render (0.7 s): which one runs depends on how much of the picture changed (6.9 KB vs 26.8 KB). That is the 5.79"'s whole responsiveness drop; the glass's first partial is worth a look.
- **E1003 responsiveness** is one sample of each open: the scene list 1471 → 2174 ms, a scene by command 2008 → 7124 ms (its next scene changed with the samples update). Scene switches overall were faster (32.5 → 20.6 s median `scene.switch_ms`).
- **Watch: Pixel toys 48.3 → 26.2 fps is not the build.** An A/B on the watch with the same scene gives 26 fps on both `7d8d073` and `1ba47e0`; the scene keeps the effect it shows between visits, and the bench's taps leave it on another one.
- **5.79": its HOME key opened the settings** before the run: its NVS held an event map from its bring-up builds, which #1099 does not take for its own. `set hardware elecrow_crowpanel_5in79` and a restart gave it the firmware's (HOME pressed: the scene list, 2.2 s).
