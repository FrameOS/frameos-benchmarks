# main ad692482e (after 2026.11.14)

A full bench of main at `ad692482e` (104 commits after the 2026.11.14 release) on every ESP32 attached to the dev box rig, on 2026-11-25. It is compared with the 2026.11.14 release's full run (`20261122-033058`, [2026.11.14.md](2026.11.14.md)).

- **Run:** `20261125-104606`, `bench.py run --rediscover --full --frames sensecap,e1002,e1003,e1004,amoled --baseline 20261122-033058`, 2 h 20 min.
- **Boards:** SenseCAP Indicator D1, reTerminal E1002, E1003 and E1004, and the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75 (its first run on this rig). All five ran images built locally from main (`firmware/build.sh`): `2026.11.14-main-ad692482e`, ELF `07052983` (generic) and `738e61ce` (32 MB). The Sticky and the Sunton were not plugged in. Cloud-W was booked by another session, and the Pico is no ESP32.
- **Backend:** the bench backend at main `ad692482e`, with both of main's migrations applied (the `store` permission and the cloud link scope) and a wasm runtime built from main.
- **Suites:** status, reboot, benchmark, scenes, builtin, input, desktop, code, settings, storm, soak and offline, with nothing sampled. The settings suite is new since the release's run, so it has no baseline.
- **Verdict as printed:** 28 crash, 5 fail, 22 slow, 6 note. **Real:** one crash (the AMOLED's Sketchbook running out of memory, repeated), a backend link off after every boot, and a slow restart under an animation. The rest of the "crash" lines and all the fails are bench bugs, described under "Not regressions". All of it is fixed in frameos#1054 (see "Fixed").
- **Recorded as `partial`** (`--frames`), so the run has no stability score: the double-counted boots would have scored every board at 20–25 %.

## Better

- **About 50 KB more internal RAM at idle on every board** (E1003 85.8 → 134.7 KB, E1004 84.6 → 133.9 KB, E1002 62.4 → 95.5 KB, SenseCAP 46.8 → 83.6 KB). The Memory score is up 33–62 %.
- **Boot to first picture:** E1003 23.5 → 7.6 s, E1004 34.3 → 21.4 s.
- **Image** +4–5 % (E1002, SenseCAP, E1004). **UI taps** on the SenseCAP 502 → 404 ms.
- No crash on the SenseCAP or any e-paper board. Desktops (every layout, four windows each), code editor, storm and offline: no issue on any board.

## Found

1. **Sketchbook cannot open on the AMOLED with the drawing it has saved.** About 5 MB of PSRAM is gone within 2.8 s of opening it (`render aborted: out of memory ... leaked 4983288`). The board then restarts and starts on another scene, and every switch back to it does the same: once in the scenes suite, once in the storm suite, five times in the soak. The saved drawing (read off the board's `/state` partition afterwards) is 94 strokes: 89 dots from the bench's taps, a 988-point pen line and a 713-point eraser line 21.4 px wide. That eraser line alone held 6 MB on the host: pixie outlined a stroke as a rectangle per flattened segment plus a whole circle at every point for a round join, and filled the whole outline at once. **Fixed** (pixie): a round join is a small polygon on the circle's arc, and an opaque stroke is filled 256 outline polygons at a time. The drawing now opens on the AMOLED with 4.15 MB free; on the host it holds 3.8 MB instead of 9.7 and renders in 163 ms instead of 524.
2. **After a boot the backend link stays off for one scene interval.** A boot's first pass makes its round trips on the render task, and the link task was switched on only at the next pass. On the E1003, reproduced after a rotation restart: one pull at the boot, then 49 s without one (its clock's interval). Pokes were still pulled at once, on the render task. **Fixed**: the link from the first render; polls every 10 s from the boot.
3. **A settings restart waits ~20 s while an LCD animates.** On the SenseCAP, rotate=0 arrived while "Slideshow with motion" ran (12 fps), and it restarted 21.7 s later. On the 1 fps Clock the same restart took 2.8 s. `settings_restart_task` (`fos_settings.c`) polled `fos_client_render_in_flight()` every 250 ms until it found a gap. Already in 2026.11.14. **Fixed**: it holds the renders and waits for the one in flight; 2.8 and 3.2 s under the slideshow.
4. **The E1002's internal RAM falls every soak round:** 93,039 → 85,295 bytes over five rounds (about 1.9 KB a round), with PSRAM flat. The release fell 2 KB in all, not every round. It has more room than before (+50 KB), but a longer soak would tell whether this ever stops. Not looked into yet.
5. Unconfirmed, one measurement each: the E1004's benchmark switch 10.2 → 16.3 s (probably refresh overlap on a 35 s panel), "Flights above me" fastest warm render 4.4 → 7.5 s (live data), and the E1002's boot to first picture 12.7 → 17.0 s.

## Not regressions

All four are fixed in the bench (frameos#1054).

- **`restart_loop` "2 boots for one restart" (12 crash lines) is a bench bug.** `console.BOOT_PATTERN` matches both `rst:0x…` and `ESP-IDF … 2nd stage bootloader`, so every ESP32 boot is counted twice. It is also why each `reboot` incident shows up twice. Every board booted once per restart. Now one boot per 5 s.
- **The E1003's scene switcher "ignoring a drag" is a bench bug.** Its 1872x1404 screen holds all 23 scenes with room to spare, so a drag has nothing to scroll and correctly draws nothing; the next picture was the switcher's own 300 s timeout. The bench now records such a round as `fits` (a later page that draws nothing is a fail).
- **The three `layout_push` fails are a bench bug.** Each pushed the set the board already held: the E1003's scenes all wore "Front page" already, and the E1002 and E1004 hold no layout, so "none" was their set as it was. The board acked the push within a second (the backend's command table) and had nothing to take, and the bench waited 300 s for a load. An earlier version of this page said the boards stopped pulling after the restart; they did not. Such a push is now skipped and recorded as `unchanged`.
- **SenseCAP Pixel toys 34.1 → 19.7 fps** (the compare's `fps_regression`): measured again on the same build after the run, 30.8 fps (43 ms a frame).
- **E1003 Graphics 7135 → 2006** (†): the scoring now takes a lower-is-better time as at least a twentieth of its reference (`scoring.LOWER_FLOOR`): 6294 on the release, 6334 on main.
- **E1004 `render_slow` (9) and `code_render_slow` (3):** its 35 s Spectra refresh, the same as on the release (code editor 23/33/35 s then, 26/33/35 s now).
- `restart_notice` notes: on the E1003 the notice once said `no-memory` (rotate 90), and `ok` otherwise.

## Fixed

In frameos#1054, each checked on the rig:

- **pixie:** a round join is a small polygon on its arc, and an opaque stroke is filled 256 outline polygons at a time. The AMOLED opens the Sketchbook with its saved drawing (5.3 s the first time, all the strokes painted; 80 ms after; 4.16 MB free). +2.3 KB of image.
- **ESP32:** the backend link from a boot's first render (the E1003 polls every 10 s from the boot), and a settings restart that holds the renders (the SenseCAP restarts 2.8–3.2 s after the settings arrive under the slideshow, 21.7 s before).
- **The bench:** one boot per restart, a switcher list that fits (`fits`), layout pushes that change nothing (`unchanged`), a time under a twentieth of its reference (`scoring.LOWER_FLOOR`), the restart notice's `ok`, and a switcher opened in a burst's quiet frame. The settings suite on the E1003 with them: no issue.
