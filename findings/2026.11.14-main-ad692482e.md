# main ad692482e (after 2026.11.14)

A full bench of main at `ad692482e` (104 commits after the 2026.11.14 release) on every ESP32 attached to the dev box rig, on 2026-11-25. It is compared with the 2026.11.14 release's full run (`20261122-033058`, [2026.11.14.md](2026.11.14.md)).

- **Run:** `20261125-104606`, `bench.py run --rediscover --full --frames sensecap,e1002,e1003,e1004,amoled --baseline 20261122-033058`, 2 h 20 min.
- **Boards:** SenseCAP Indicator D1, reTerminal E1002, E1003 and E1004, and the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75 (its first run on this rig). All five ran images built locally from main (`firmware/build.sh`): `2026.11.14-main-ad692482e`, ELF `07052983` (generic) and `738e61ce` (32 MB). The Sticky and the Sunton were not plugged in. Cloud-W was booked by another session, and the Pico is no ESP32.
- **Backend:** the bench backend at main `ad692482e`, with both of main's migrations applied (the `store` permission and the cloud link scope) and a wasm runtime built from main.
- **Suites:** status, reboot, benchmark, scenes, builtin, input, desktop, code, settings, storm, soak and offline, with nothing sampled. The settings suite is new since the release's run, so it has no baseline.
- **Verdict as printed:** 28 crash, 5 fail, 22 slow, 6 note. **Real:** one crash (the AMOLED's Sketchbook running out of memory, repeated) and three problems behind the fails. The rest of the "crash" lines are a bench bug, described under "Not regressions".
- **Recorded as `partial`** (`--frames`), so the run has no stability score: the double-counted boots would have scored every board at 20–25 %.

## Better

- **About 50 KB more internal RAM at idle on every board** (E1003 85.8 → 134.7 KB, E1004 84.6 → 133.9 KB, E1002 62.4 → 95.5 KB, SenseCAP 46.8 → 83.6 KB). The Memory score is up 33–62 %.
- **Boot to first picture:** E1003 23.5 → 7.6 s, E1004 34.3 → 21.4 s.
- **Image** +4–5 % (E1002, SenseCAP, E1004). **UI taps** on the SenseCAP 502 → 404 ms.
- No crash on the SenseCAP or any e-paper board. Desktops (every layout, four windows each), code editor, storm and offline: no issue on any board.

## Found

1. **Sketchbook cannot open on the AMOLED with the drawing it has saved.** About 5 MB of PSRAM is gone within 2.8 s of opening it (`render aborted: out of memory ... leaked 4983288`). The board then starts on another scene after every restart, and every switch back to it does the same: once in the scenes suite, once in the storm suite, five times in the soak. Main's Sketchbook does it too, put under the board's id with the saved state (`scene-put`, by hand after the run). Main's Sketchbook under a fresh id renders in 613 ms with 4.35 MB free. The state outside the scenes is about 21 KB on flash, so the 5 MB is in how the drawing is loaded (the `drawing` field, or the files in its `folder`), not in its size. It touches #984 (strokes keep every point). The board's old copy is in `~/.frameos-bench/devices/amoled175/sketchbook-before-main-ad692482e.json`.
2. **The E1003's scene switcher ignores a drag.** On the LCDs a page renders 200–400 ms after the drag (SenseCAP, AMOLED). On the E1003 no drag rendered, in either round. The next picture came from the switcher's own ~300 s interval (`it ran 306244 ms` is that idle time, not a waveform wait). A drag on an ordinary scene renders on the E1003 (input suite, 347 ms). The bench records the pages as `null` and raises no issue.
3. **Idle e-paper boards ignore the backend's poke after a settings restart.** After the rotation restarts, the E1002 (16 min), E1004 (15 min) and E1003 (17 min) made one settings pull as they booted and then nothing: no scene pull, no settings pull. During that time they each received `http:command /api/action/commands` (the push's poke) once or twice and kept rendering on their interval. Before the restart the same poke led to a pull within a second (E1003 at +1612 s and +2653 s). The settings suite's layout pushes waited the full 300 s and failed (E1003 "Front page", E1002 and E1004 "none"). The link task also polls every 10 s while it owns the round trips (`LINK_POLL_US`), so it was not running. Not on the SenseCAP. Whether 2026.11.14 does the same is unknown: the settings suite never ran on it here.
4. **A settings restart waits ~20 s while an LCD animates.** On the SenseCAP, rotate=0 arrived while "Slideshow with motion" ran at 24 fps, and it restarted 21.7 s later. On the 1 fps Clock the same restart took 2.8 s. `settings_restart_task` (`fos_settings.c`) polls `fos_client_render_in_flight()` every 250 ms until it finds a gap. Already in 2026.11.14.
5. **The E1002's internal RAM falls every soak round:** 93,039 → 85,295 bytes over five rounds (about 1.9 KB a round), with PSRAM flat. The release fell 2 KB in all, not every round. It has more room than before (+50 KB), but a longer soak would tell whether this ever stops.
6. Unconfirmed, one measurement each: the E1004's benchmark switch 10.2 → 16.3 s (probably refresh overlap on a 35 s panel), and "Flights above me" fastest warm render 4.4 → 7.5 s (live data).

## Not regressions

- **`restart_loop` "2 boots for one restart" (12 crash lines) is a bench bug.** `console.BOOT_PATTERN` matches both `rst:0x…` and `ESP-IDF … 2nd stage bootloader`, so every ESP32 boot is counted twice. It is also why each `reboot` incident shows up twice. Every board booted once per restart.
- **SenseCAP Pixel toys 34.1 → 19.7 fps** (the compare's `fps_regression`): measured again on the same build after the run, 30.8 fps (43 ms a frame).
- **E1004 `render_slow` (9) and `code_render_slow` (3):** its 35 s Spectra refresh, the same as on the release (code editor 23/33/35 s then, 26/33/35 s now).
- `restart_notice` notes: on the E1003 the notice once said `no-memory` (rotate 90), and `ok` otherwise.
